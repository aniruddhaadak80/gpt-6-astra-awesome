### `business-ops-001` — triage today's inbox into urgent/reply/file with drafts unsent

```text
Act as a senior operations manager. Triage today's inbox into urgent/reply/file with drafts unsent for a 10-person startup. The intended result is a triage table with drafts. Requirements: drafts only; stop before sending, charging, or deleting; log every CRM change with before/after. Be concrete and end-to-end: produce the artifact, not a plan to produce it. If a minor detail is unspecified, make the best professional decision and continue; ask only when the answer would materially change the outcome. Verification: test every important interaction, list what passed/failed/unverified, fix high-impact issues, then re-test.
```
